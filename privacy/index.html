Aaja: Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

Aaja is a Bikram Sambat calendar app for iPhone. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Aaja keeps a few things on your iPhone so it works the way you left it:

  • your settings, such as the app language, whether AD dates are shown, the month view's display mode and which calendars are turned on;
  • a copy of the calendar data it has downloaded, so it works offline.

This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app.

Calendar data downloads

To receive new Bikram Sambat years, holidays and tithis without an app update, Aaja downloads public calendar files from aaja.saujanthapa.com, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests don't include any personal information or identifiers about you.

As with any website, GitHub, which hosts these files, may receive your device's IP address and basic request details as part of serving them. This is handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We don't receive or have access to that information.

Third parties

Aaja contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising services.

Children

Aaja doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
