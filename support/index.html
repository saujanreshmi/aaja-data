Aaja: Support

Aaja is a Bikram Sambat calendar for iPhone, with tithis, holidays and Nepal time.

Contact

For help, feedback or to report a wrong date, holiday or tithi, email aaja@saujanthapa.com.

Calendar data

Aaja checks for new calendar data automatically. To fetch the latest holidays right away, open the Calendars sheet from the calendar button and pull down to refresh.

Privacy

Aaja doesn't collect any personal information. See the privacy policy.
